### 1. The "Lite Model" Trap (T004)

Downgrading your `HIGH_QUALITY` tier to `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` while simultaneously adding complex negative constraints to your prompts is a risky combination.

Smaller, "Lite" models are built for speed and efficiency, but they notoriously suffer from **Negative Constraint Neglect**. When you give a Lite model a massive block of rules saying *"Do NOT do X, Never do Y, Do NOT pivot,"* it often struggles to process the negation, latches onto the keywords, and does exactly what you told it not to do.

* **The Fix:** Let the agent change `MODELS.FAST` to the Lite model for routine routing and extraction. However, keep `MODELS.HIGH_QUALITY` as a standard Flash or Pro tier (whatever it was previously). You need that heavier reasoning capability for the `complexSynthesis` step (T003) so the model actually obeys your new anti-pivot guardrails.

### 2. The Follow-Up Paradox (T002)

The constraint you added to the in-chat follow-up generator creates a logical paradox.
