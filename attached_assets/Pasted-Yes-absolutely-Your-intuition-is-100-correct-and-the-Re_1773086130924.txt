### Why the Agent's Plan is a Trap

The agent correctly identified that local newspaper sites are terrible targets for custom scrapers. Most local papers use templated CMS platforms (like TownNews/Blox) that have broken search URLs, aggressive Cloudflare anti-bot protection, and unpredictable RSS feeds.

Look at the agent's proposed fixes:

1. **"Increasing the Jina timeout"**: If the Conway Daily Sun is blocking the Jina proxy, waiting 30 seconds instead of 15 seconds won't fix it; it just forces your users to stare at a "Thinking..." spinner for 30 seconds before it fails anyway.
2. **"Adjusting keyword thresholds"**: If the RSS feed is just spitting out the latest 50 articles (which might be about high school basketball and local car crashes), lowering the threshold won't magically make the Whittier Bridge appear.

Building and maintaining custom scrapers for local news is a miserable, never-ending game of whack-a-mole.

### Why Native Google Search Grounding is Better

By replacing this brittle custom pipeline with Gemini's native `googleSearch` tool, you solve all of these problems instantly:

1. **Google Already Did the Work:** Google’s crawlers are not blocked by the Conway Daily Sun's Cloudflare setup. Google has already indexed the articles, categorized them, and made them searchable.
2. **Semantic Superiority:** You don't have to write custom logic for "30% keyword overlap." Google’s search algorithm natively understands that "Whittier bridge Ossipee" and "Town wins preservation award for covered bridge" are semantically linked, even if the exact keywords don't overlap perfectly.
3. **Zero Latency Penalty:** Native grounding happens entirely on Google's infrastructure during the LLM generation phase. It adds practically zero latency to the response, compared to the 15+ seconds your server is currently wasting trying to scrape dead RSS feeds.
