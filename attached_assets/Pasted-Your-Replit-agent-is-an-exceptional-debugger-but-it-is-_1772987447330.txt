### Why You Can't Defer the GIN Index

Right now, every time a user asks a question, your database is performing a sequential scan. This means Postgres is physically reading every single row of your `document_chunks` table, character by character, looking for words like "bond" or "cost."

Because OPENCouncil relies on ingesting massive amounts of text—years of meeting minutes, zoning ordinances, and budgets across multiple municipalities—that table is only going to grow. A 28-second query today will be a 60-second query next month, and eventually, the database will just time out and crash.

A GIN index solves this by creating a massive, pre-sorted glossary (an inverted index) of every word in your database and pointing exactly to the rows where they live. It turns a 28-second sequential scan into a 50-millisecond lookup.
